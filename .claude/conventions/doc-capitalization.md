# Capitalization for `content/doc/`

- `title`, `linkTitle` and `##` headings use **Chicago title case**. H3 and lower headings are left as they are.
  - Capitalize the first and last word, the first word after a colon, nouns, pronouns, verbs (including *Is*, *Are*, *Be*), adjectives, adverbs and subordinating conjunctions (*If*, *When*, *That*). Capitalize verb particles as well (*Set Up*, *Pays Off*) and both parts of hyphenated compounds (*Real-Time*).
  - Lowercase articles (*a*, *an*, *the*), the coordinating conjunctions *and*, *but*, *or*, *nor*, *for*, *so* and *yet*, **all prepositions regardless of length** (*with*, *from*, *about*, *vs.*), *to* and *as*.
  - Keep product names, acronyms and file names exactly as written (*CLAUDE.md*, *MCP*, *GitHub Actions*).
  - Examples: "What Is Claude Code?", "Share What You Build with Your Team", "Trust It: Verifying Unsupervised Runs".
